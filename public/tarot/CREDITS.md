# Tarot card images

`rws/*.webp` — Rider–Waite–Smith tarot deck (1909), illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith, published by William Rider & Son.
Public domain. Scans from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck),
resized to 480px wide and converted to WebP by `scripts/tarot/fetch-images.mjs`.

The card back is an original CSS design (`src/components/tarot/card-face.tsx`).
